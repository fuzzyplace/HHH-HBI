Human Brain Interface

Come back to yourself

A few quiet minutes to settle your nervous system. Your brainwaves and heartbeat paint living ink and play the sound around you. All you do is notice, breathe and rest.

How long

Headphones help. Art and meditation, not a medical device. Your brain and heart signals never leave this device.

Step 1 of 3 · Put it on

Wear your Muse 2

  1. Switch it on. The light starts to pulse.
  2. Rest the band on your forehead and tuck the ear pieces behind your ears, touching skin. Move hair aside.
  3. Press Connect, then choose your Muse in the list your browser shows.

Step 2 of 3 · Settle in

Let the sensors find you

Waiting for the headset signal…

Sit comfortably. Adjust the band until at least three dots glow green. If the ear dots stay red, dampen the skin behind your ears.

Step 3 of 3 · Your baseline

Meet your resting rhythm

About half a minute: first with eyes open, then with eyes closed. Your brain's alpha rhythm rises when your eyes close, so measuring both tunes everything to you. One tone means close your eyes; two tones mean open them.

Eyes open

Look softly at one point on the screen. Stay still, jaw relaxed.

Connected

This is you

Each sensor feeds ink from where it sits on your head. Your heartbeat moves the water. The chord follows your brainwaves.

Welcome back

What changed

A mirror, not a score. Bodies vary day to day.
